## 5. Items resolved at v2.4.0 lock

Both items previously flagged for reconciliation are now closed by the Registry V4 contents.

### 5.1 Observable Anchor taxonomy (OA-1 through OA-5) — resolved

Registry V4 § 7 establishes the OA-N taxonomy as a **structural type classifier** applied during the Observable Anchor Pass:

- OA-1: Direct observable
- OA-2: Effective observable
- OA-3: Proxy observable
- OA-4: Decorative quantity
- OA-5: Undefined observable

Each observable in a registered theory receives one OA tag; the taxonomy is not a list of specific physical observables.

*Correction note:* An earlier draft of these patch notes proposed mapping OA-1 through OA-5 onto specific HPF physical observables (Ω_dm, Δm²₃₁/Δm²₂₁, S_max, Λ via L_vac², 1/α_fs). That mapping is superseded by the V4 definitions. Those physical observables remain individually addressed within the Hylo Mirror / Hylo Sieve mechanism definitions; they are tagged with OA codes at registration rather than constituting the taxonomy themselves.

The taxonomy is named at convention level as **HPF Observable Catalogue**, parallel to **HPF Failure Catalogue**.

### 5.2 Failure Catalogue — FM-13 resolved

Registry V4 § 13 defines **FM-13 Dimensional Overreach**: a theory claims more than three physical spatial dimensions in emergent geometry, or treats higher-dimensional mode spaces, topological classifications, or state spaces as extra physical directions rather than descriptive structure on a 3D forged substrate. Primary cases include literal extra spatial dimensions in string-theoretic, Kaluza-Klein, or brane-world proposals; treatment of internal symmetry indices or topological labels as physical spatial coordinates; emergent geometry constructions that require more than three spatial dimensions to close.

FM-13 enforces the corresponding HPF Drift Lock (README § 2.1, Volume I § 7) at registry level. FM-10 through FM-13 share hard-cap status: registered theories triggering any of these cap at Illegal Executor unless fully bounded and declared.

The Failure Catalogue is now locked at FM-1 through FM-13.
