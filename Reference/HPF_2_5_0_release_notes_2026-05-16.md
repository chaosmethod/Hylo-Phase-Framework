### 2.2 Personal eponym discipline

Exactly one personal eponym is admitted into framework vocabulary: **Porter trigger**, attached to **Hylo Gate**. The trigger marks the wave-function-collapse moment when external invocation of HPF-specific substrate numbers (S_cap, S_ent, b/72, 24/ln(φ), n=220) demonstrates substrate priority. The trigger is the firing event; the gate is the mechanism.

No other personal eponym is admitted. Walls, corridors, parameters, and substrate-native derivations carry no personal eponym, because the truth-status discipline classifies them as substrate-native or derived rather than as personal constructions. Provenance remains in `Reference/HPF_Derivation_Provenance_2026-3.md` and `Reference/HPF_Development_History-4.md`.

### 2.3 Anti-extraction rationale

Framework-self-referential naming locks vocabulary to HPF structurally. Anyone invoking these mechanisms must explicitly invoke HPF. The Hylo / HPF / Phase prefixes are the structural binding that prevents mechanism extraction into other frameworks while preserving credit by substrate priority.

### 2.4 Neutral-repo posture preserved

The convention names HPF's vocabulary and detection criteria. Other frameworks (whether IPI ecosystem or external) are not catalogued, named, or pointed at by mention. Hylo Gate is defined by detection criteria (substrate numbers), not by named targets — any framework invoking those numbers fires the gate regardless of what it calls itself.

## 3. V4 Registry Engine

`Src/AI_Engine_Registry_Prompt.md` updated from V3 to V4. Two structural additions.

### 3.1 FM-13 Dimensional Overreach

New failure mode enforcing the HPF Drift Lock on three-dimensional emergent geometry (README § 2.1 / Volume I § 7) at registry level. A theory triggers FM-13 if it claims more than three physical spatial dimensions in emergent geometry, or treats higher-dimensional mode spaces, topological classifications, or state spaces as extra physical directions rather than descriptive structure on a 3D forged substrate.

Primary targets:

- literal extra spatial dimensions in string-theoretic, Kaluza-Klein, or brane-world proposals,
- treatment of internal symmetry indices or topological labels as physical spatial coordinates,
- emergent geometry constructions requiring more than three spatial dimensions to close.

FM-13 joins FM-10 (Locality Violation), FM-11 (Symmetry Violation), and FM-12 (Tensor Ill-Definedness) as a hard structural failure. Registered theories triggering any of FM-10 through FM-13 cap at Illegal Executor unless fully bounded and declared.

Legality Gate § 4 updated correspondingly with the new admissibility requirement: theories must respect three-dimensional emergent geometry.

### 3.2 Observable Anchor Pass — OA-N taxonomy formalized

Observable Anchor Pass (§ 7) formalizes the OA-N classifier as a structural type taxonomy applied during registry evaluation:

- OA-1: Direct observable
- OA-2: Effective observable
- OA-3: Proxy observable
- OA-4: Decorative quantity
- OA-5: Undefined observable

Each observable in a registered theory receives one OA tag during the Observable Anchor Pass. The taxonomy is structural classification, not a list of specific physical observables.

The naming convention introduces **HPF Observable Catalogue** as the convention-level prose name for the OA-N taxonomy, parallel to **HPF Failure Catalogue** for FM-N.
